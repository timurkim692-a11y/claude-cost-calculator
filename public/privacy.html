Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This policy describes how the personal application published at timurkim692-a11y.github.io/claude-cost-calculator (the “Application”), including its Google Drive and Google Sheets connector, handles user data. The Application is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by its developer for their own use.

Data we access

When you sign in with Google and grant permission, the Application may access the Google Drive files and Google Sheets spreadsheets covered by the scopes you approve, along with basic account information (your email address) needed to identify the signed-in account.

How we use data

  • Data is used only to perform the actions you request, such as reading, searching or updating your files and spreadsheets.
  • Data is not used for advertising, profiling, or to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • Data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties, except where required by law.

Storage and security

The Application does not store your Google data on its own servers. OAuth tokens are kept only on the device that runs the connector and are used solely to call Google APIs on your behalf. The cost calculator web page itself collects no personal data and uses no cookies or analytics.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The Application’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke the Application’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation the Application can no longer access your data.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted via GitHub Issues.
